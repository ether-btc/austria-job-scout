## Residential-IP Safety Verified

The `_find_jrf_path()` change in H-3 directly implements the user preference (importance 1.00) about not fetching hundreds of jobs at once through the residential IP. When the JRF/curl_cffi stealth module is unavailable, the user gets a clear WARNING before they accidentally scrape CF-protected sites with vanilla requests. This is now a contract: **no silent fallbacks** in the residential-IP path.
